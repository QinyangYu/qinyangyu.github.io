<section class="block" id="bio">
  <h2 class="block-title">Short Bio</h2>
  <div class="block-body">
    <p>Hi! Welcome to my website!</p>
    <p>I am Qinyang Yu, a first-year PhD student at the <a href="https://datascience.virginia.edu/">School of Data Science</a>, <a href="https://www.virginia.edu/">University of Virginia</a>. I am co-advised by <a href="https://yyahn.com/about/">YY Ahn</a> and <a href="https://datascience.virginia.edu/people/alex-gates">Alex Gates</a>, and I am a member of <a href="https://yyahn.com/ylab/">Y Lab</a>.</p>
    <p>My research focuses on the dynamics of complex social systems—from human belief systems to the behaviors of large language models, and from the spread of information to the evolution of culture. Methodologically, I leverage tools from computational social science, network science, natural language processing, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.</p>
  </div>
</section>
